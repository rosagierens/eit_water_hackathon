## Info
Data is provided on unit level.

For each unit, probably relevant data is (copied from data file): 
- Project Name: Commonly used name
- Latitude: decimal degrees; Latitude, assumed WGS84 (google maps)
- Longitude: decimal degrees; Latitude, assumed WGS84 (google maps)
- Capacity (MW): Nameplate capacity, or best available data, of the unit in megawatts
- Reference Net Capacity (MW): Net capacity of the unit for the reference period
- Design Net Capacity (MW): Net capacity of the unit as originally designed by the manufacturer
- Thermal Capacity (MWt): Net capacity for heat transferred from nuclear fuel to coolant
- GEM location ID: GEM-generated ID unique for each project

## Data processing

Filter to only include plants with Status "operating", and Region "Europe"
No columns dropped.
